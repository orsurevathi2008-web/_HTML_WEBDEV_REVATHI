Paradha

Genre:Drama and social Drama

review

Paradha is a meaningful social drama with strong performances, especially from Anupama Parameswaran and Darshana Rajendran. ([Cinema Express][1]) The film has a bold theme and beautiful visuals, though the pacing and execution feel uneven in places.

Rating
